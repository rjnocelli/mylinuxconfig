Ctrl + Alt + RightArrow ---- move to right workspace
Ctrl + Alt + LeftArrow ---- move to left workspace

AltGr + Shift + Number (1,2,3,4) ---- move to workspace number (1,2,3,4)

Shift + Super + UpArrow ---- Move to App to right/up monitor
Shift + Super + DownArrow ---- Move to App to left/down monitor

Ctrl + Space ---- Search Bar

Super + T ---- Tilding Options
